Final degree thesis (TFG)

Improving the Knowledge Management System of a Technology Company through Artificial Intelligence

The thesis focuses on the organizational knowledge lifecycle, which I study across four stages:

Acquiringnew knowledge
Applyingexisting knowledge
Retainingcurrent knowledge
Managingobsolete or invalid knowledge

Note on method: agentic AI applications are used as a tool to speed up the research itself — not as the object of study.
